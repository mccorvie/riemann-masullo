\section*{Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Introduction}

While formalizing A.\ M.\ Masullo's \emph{The Music of the Primes} in Lean,
we sometimes have to supply a missing argument or find a different proof.
This companion records some of those arguments so they can be read and
discussed alongside the paper.

The notes follow the order of the paper, one result at a time. Each states
the result, gives the proof in mathematical prose, and links to the
corresponding Lean declarations. We explain changes to the printed
argument and identify any open steps the proof uses.
