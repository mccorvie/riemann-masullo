# Errata and gaps found in the paper

Problems found in A. M. Masullo, *The Music of the Primes*, while turning it into Lean. They
refer to the draft of 20 November 2026 (sha256 in [`paper/README.md`](paper/README.md)).

Most entries were first reported by an AI agent. The **Checked** column says whether someone has
since read the passage and agrees: *integrator* means the integrating agent compared the report
with the page; *reviewed* means a second, independent agent recomputed it against the page image; *reported*
means nobody has yet. No entry has been reviewed by a specialist. The
dispute over the main theorem is described in the [README](README.md#concerns-about-the-main-theorem), not here.

The author's preface says that he and Brian Conrad know of "several glitches here and there"
still being fixed, and that the draft is unfinished. Most entries are in the appendices
and early chapters that recall standard material. Since the second wave (Appendix B.4–B.6 and
§5.2, entries F19–F44, I100–I135 and M8–M16; then §5.2.2 to §7.1.4, entries F45–F104, I136–I218
and M17–M20) many concern the paper's own constructions: the proof of the structure theorem for
the spaces of adelic θ-functions (Theorem 5.2.1.1) does not work as printed, the explicit
formulas of §B.4, which Chapter 12 quotes, need their local terms and normalizations recomputed,
and several steps of Chapters 5–7 have no known repair (listed next). None of the entries is
about the trace formula itself.

## Where no repair is known yet

Most entries below come with a repair, and the Lean states the repaired form. For these the
agents that checked the entries found no repair, and later results use them. That is their
assessment, not a specialist's; each is a candidate for a recorded gap (`AGENTS.md`, "False
statements and gaps") unless someone proves it, repairs it or refutes it.

## How this file is kept

Digesters and provers mark a problem where they meet it with a comment `-- TODO(paper-gap): …`
in the Lean file and describe it in their report. When the pull request is merged, the integrator
reads the passage and adds an entry here. `grep -rn "TODO(paper-gap)" RiemannMasullo` lists every
mark in the code.

Contributors report what they find on the issue: a counterexample, a mismatch between the Lean
and the print, or a step of a printed proof that fails. The integrator checks each report against
the page and decides what becomes of the statement ([`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md), "False statements
and gaps").

A statement that is false as printed is also kept in Lean, in the section's `_Printed.lean`
file: the printed claim as a proposition `Printed.<name>`, and a theorem `Printed.<name>_false`
that proves its negation. Where that proof is finished, the entry's **Checked** column says
*Lean*: such an entry rests on the proof checker, not on a reading. F2 is the first
(`RiemannMasullo/AppA_AlgebraicNT/Sec1_Printed.lean`); the entries made before this rule are
being brought into this form, and each row names its proposition once it exists.

A statement that can be neither proved nor refuted is recorded as a gap: a named theorem left
unproved, with a `Gap:` line in its docstring and an issue labelled `gap`. Gaps are listed under
"Placeholders and missing material".
